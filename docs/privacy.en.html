Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Luò collects no data about you.

Luò does not use the network

There is no networking code in Luò. Casting, reading the coins, the classical text, the plain-language gloss, the cast log — every feature runs offline on your device. There is no server and nothing is uploaded.

What Luò does not collect

Luò has no accounts and requires no sign-up or sign-in. It contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), builds no user profile, and shares nothing with third parties — because there is nothing to share.

Where your cast log lives

Your cast log (the hexagram, changing lines, timestamp, and any question or note you type yourself) is stored inside your device's app sandbox and never leaves the device.

You can swipe to delete individual casts, or clear them all, from the Cast Log screen. Deleting Luò removes every record with it.

Motion data

Luò requests Motion & Fitness access to read accelerometer data, which drives the physics of the coin throw. That data is used in memory as it arrives. It is never stored and never transmitted.

What Apple may give us

If you opt in under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymized, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. That sharing is controlled by you in system settings, can be turned off at any time, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Luò is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.
